# Handoff Card — when a real cross-context transfer is needed

TASK_ID: <selected task>
MODE: <EXECUTE | AUDIT | REVIEW | VERIFY | DIAGNOSE>
TARGET: <repository/ref/PR/Issue or artifact>
AUTHORITY: <accepted task + actual user/host authorization locator>
BOUNDARY: <bounded read/write scope, risk/effects and relevant exclusions>
DELTA: <current action or findings not already covered by linked sources>
RETURN_CONTRACT: <decisive outcome and evidence to report>

Optional: `CAPABILITY` for a needed specialist Skill; `CARD_ID` for traceability.

The receiving context validates its actual current permissions. Link the accepted Issue/Task, design or files rather than copying their contents. Read-only modes do not authorize mutations.
